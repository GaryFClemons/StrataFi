Espresso and Brew Standards

Version 6. Effective August 1, 2026. Owner: Director of Coffee. Approved by the Leadership Team on July 13, 2026. Replaces version 5.

1 Dialing in

Dial in every grinder each morning before the café opens, and again whenever two shots in a row run outside the recipe's time range. Bring shots back into range by changing the grind, not the dose. Only a Shift Lead or a Level 3 barista may change a recipe's dose, by no more than 0.5 g and only for that day, recorded on the dial-in log. Weigh every espresso dose and yield on the bar scales.

3 Drink builds

Small (8 oz) and medium (12 oz) hot drinks get a double shot, and large (16 oz) hot drinks get a triple. Iced drinks get a double shot in every size. Cold brew is served as one part concentrate to one part water over ice, unless the guest asks for it stronger.

4 Milk

Steam milk to between 140°F and 155°F (60–68°C). "Extra hot" means up to 170°F (77°C), and kids' drinks are steamed to 120°F (49°C). Never steam the same milk twice. Latte foam is about 1 cm deep in the cup, and cappuccino foam about 2 cm.

5 Batch brew holding

Batch brew is held in its thermal server for no more than 30 minutes, then poured out, even if the server is still full. After 2:00 p.m., or whenever fewer than about 10 cups an hour are selling, brew half batches. Write each server's brew time on its tag.

6 Beans

Use espresso beans between 7 and 28 days after their roast date, and filter beans between 4 and 21 days after it. Beans past those windows can go into cold brew up to 45 days after roasting; after that they go back to the roastery on the next delivery truck. Fill hoppers with no more than one day's beans, and keep open bags folded and clipped.

7 Quality checks

A District Manager or Coffee Trainer checks each café's coffee every month: two shots from each grinder, and a batch brew measured with a refractometer. Batch brew should read 1.30–1.45% TDS. A café that falls outside a range at two checks in a row gets a visit from the Director of Coffee's team within 14 days.
