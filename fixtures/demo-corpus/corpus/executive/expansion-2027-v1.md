# 2027 Expansion Plan

Version 1. Effective October 20, 2026. From the Chief Executive Officer and the Chief Financial Officer to the executive team. Approved by the board on October 14, 2026. Confidential: executive team only.

## 1 Summary

In 2027 Halcyon will open seven cafés and close one, add a second roasting line at Fenwick Street, and grow wholesale revenue by a quarter. The plan is paid for from operating cash and an equipment lease, with no new outside investment.

## 2 New cafés

Six full cafés open in 2027: three in Port Alder (Millrace, Eastgate, and the Canal Market) and three in Riverton, our first new city. A seventh site, a kiosk in Concourse B of Port Alder International Airport, opens in the fall. Each full café has a build-out budget of $850,000 and the kiosk $420,000. Riverton becomes its own district, and its District Manager is hired by March 1, 2027.

## 3 Harbor Point closure

The Harbor Point café closes when its lease ends on June 30, 2027; the landlord's renewal terms would double the rent. Its staff will be told on April 1, 2027, and every employee there will be offered a role at another café at the same pay. Until then, the closure isn't discussed outside the executive team.

## 4 Roastery

A second roasting line, built around a 60-kilo roaster, will be running by August 2027. It raises the roastery's capacity from 1.2 million to 2 million pounds a year, at a capital cost of $1.4 million.

## 5 Wholesale

The wholesale revenue target for 2027 is $9.5 million, up 25%. Two sales reps will be added, and a grocery program for 12 oz bags launches in the second quarter.

## 6 Prices

Drink prices rise by an average of 4% on March 1, 2027. Café staff hear about it two weeks before. Whole-bean coffee prices stay where they are.

## 7 Budget and hiring

Capital spending totals $7.52 million: $5.1 million for the six cafés, $420,000 for the kiosk, $1.4 million for the roasting line, and $600,000 to refit existing cafés. About 150 people will be hired, most of them baristas for the new cafés.

## 8 Confidentiality

Don't share this plan or anything in it outside the executive team. Café Managers will be briefed on the parts that affect them, in stages, starting in January 2027.
