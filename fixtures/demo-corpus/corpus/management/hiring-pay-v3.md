# Hiring and Pay Bands

Version 3. Effective January 1, 2026. Owner: Director of People. Approved by the Leadership Team on December 8, 2025. Replaces version 2. For Assistant Café Managers and above.

## 1 Opening a position

A Café Manager requests a new or replacement position in the HR system, and the District Manager approves it. Café roles are posted internally for 5 days before they're posted publicly, and internal applicants who meet the requirements are always interviewed.

## 2 Interviews

Barista candidates have a 30-minute interview with the Café Manager or an Assistant Café Manager, then a paid 2-hour working interview on bar with a Coffee Trainer. Shift Lead and manager candidates also have a second interview with the District Manager. Use the questions in the interview guide, and never ask about age, family plans, health, religion, or immigration status.

## 3 Pay bands

| Role | Pay type | Band minimum | Band maximum |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Barista, Level 1 | Hourly | $17.50 | $18.50 |
| Barista, Level 2 | Hourly | $18.50 | $20.25 |
| Senior Barista, Level 3 | Hourly | $19.75 | $22.00 |
| Shift Lead | Hourly | $21.00 | $24.00 |
| Assistant Café Manager | Hourly | $24.50 | $28.00 |
| Café Manager | Salary | $58,000 | $72,000 |
| District Manager | Salary | $84,000 | $102,000 |
| Production Roaster | Hourly | $23.00 | $30.00 |

## 4 Making an offer

Offers start at the band minimum. A Café Manager can offer up to the middle of the band to a candidate with at least a year of specialty coffee experience. An offer above the middle of the band needs the District Manager's approval, and an offer above the band maximum needs the Director of People. Offers are made in writing through the HR system, never by text message alone.

## 5 Background checks

Candidates for Shift Lead and above have a background check after the offer and before their first shift. A record doesn't automatically rule anyone out; the People Team decides case by case.

## 6 Referral bonus

An employee who refers a new hire receives $300 once a referred barista completes 90 days, or $750 for a Shift Lead or manager hire. Managers don't receive referral bonuses for hires into their own café.

## 7 Pay reviews

Pay is reviewed every March. Merit increases range from 0% to 4% of base pay, depending on the year's review, and never take anyone above their band maximum. Level raises under the Barista Training and Certification Program are separate and take effect when they're earned.
