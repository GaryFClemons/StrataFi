# Performance and Discipline Guide

Version 2. Effective October 1, 2025. Owner: Director of People. Approved by the Leadership Team on September 8, 2025. Replaces version 1. For Assistant Café Managers and above.

## 1 Coaching comes first

Most problems are solved by a direct, private conversation. Record each coaching conversation in the HR system within 48 hours: the date, what was discussed, and what was agreed. Coaching isn't discipline and doesn't need the People Team's approval.

## 2 Progressive discipline

When coaching hasn't worked, discipline has four steps: a documented verbal warning, a written warning, a final written warning, and termination. A verbal warning stays active for 6 months and a written warning for 12 months; after that, a new problem starts at whatever step the manager judges fits. Café Managers issue verbal and written warnings, and a final written warning needs the District Manager's approval.

## 3 Attendance points

| Event | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Clocking in 6 or more minutes late | 0.5 |
| Leaving early without approval | 0.5 |
| Calling out less than 2 hours before a shift, except for illness | 1 |
| No-call, no-show | 3 |

Points stay on record for a rolling 12 months. At 6 points the Café Manager issues a written warning, at 8 points a final written warning, and at 10 points the case goes to the People Team for a termination review. Absences covered by sick time, approved PTO, or protected leave never earn points.

## 4 Serious misconduct

Theft, violence or threats, harassment, working under the influence, and serious food safety violations can lead to termination on the first offense. Suspend the employee with pay for up to 5 working days while the People Team investigates, and don't fire anyone on the spot.

## 5 Performance improvement plans

A performance improvement plan lasts 30, 60, or 90 days, sets measurable goals, and is approved by the District Manager before it's given to the employee. Meet weekly while the plan runs, and record each meeting.

## 6 Terminations

Only the District Manager, with the People Team's approval, can end someone's employment. The one exception is the resignation rule for three consecutive no-call, no-show shifts in the Scheduling and Shift Swap Policy. Final pay follows state law, and the People Team arranges the exit conversation.
