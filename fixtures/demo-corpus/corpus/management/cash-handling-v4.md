# Cash Handling and Safe Procedures

Version 4. Effective March 15, 2026. Owner: Director of Café Operations. Approved by the Leadership Team on February 23, 2026. Replaces version 3. For Assistant Café Managers and above.

## 1 Who handles cash

Only Shift Leads, Assistant Café Managers, and Café Managers set up, count, and close tills. Baristas take cash only at a till assigned to them for the shift, and nobody works from another person's till. The safe has two compartments: the float compartment, which Shift Leads open with their own codes, and the main compartment, which only Café Managers and Assistant Café Managers can open.

## 2 Till floats

Each till starts the day with a $150 float, in the notes and coins the till sheet lists. The opening Shift Lead counts each float out of the float compartment with a second person, and both sign the till sheet.

## 3 Counts and variances

### 3.1 When tills are counted

Tills are counted at every change of cashier and at close, always by two people, one of them a Shift Lead or manager. The count goes into the register's cash-up screen before the drawer is closed.

### 3.2 Variance limits

A till that is over or short by $5 or less is recorded, and nothing more is needed. Over $5, the two counters count again, and the Shift Lead writes the likely reason on the till sheet. A variance over $20, or a third variance over $5 by the same cashier within 30 days, is reported to the Café Manager the same day and to the District Manager within 24 hours. Shortages are never taken out of anyone's pay.

## 4 Safe drops

When a till holds more than $300 above its float, the cashier asks for a safe drop. A Shift Lead or manager takes out the extra notes, counts them with the cashier, and drops them into the main compartment in a sealed bag, and both sign the drop log.

## 5 The safe

The main compartment opens only after a 10-minute time delay; tell anyone who asks that no one in the café can open the safe quickly. Apart from the till floats and a $400 change fund, the safe never holds more than $2,500. Change the codes within 24 hours after anyone who knows them leaves Halcyon or moves to another café, and at least every 6 months.

## 6 Cash collection

An armored courier collects the cash deposit every Monday, Wednesday, and Friday. When the safe would pass its $2,500 limit before the next collection, the Café Manager books an extra pickup through the cash portal that day. Two people prepare each deposit bag, both sign the collection log, and the courier's receipt is photographed and uploaded to the cash portal the same day.

## 7 Counterfeit notes

Check every $50 note with the counterfeit pen and the light by the register. Halcyon cafés don't accept $100 notes. If a note looks fake, politely decline it and ask for another way to pay; don't keep the note or accuse the guest.

## 8 After a robbery

Follow the robbery steps in Workplace Safety and Injury Reporting. Once the police have finished, close the café, count the remaining cash with a second person, and send the count and the incident report to the District Manager and the Director of Café Operations before the end of the day.
