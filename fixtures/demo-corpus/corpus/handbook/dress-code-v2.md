# Dress Code and Appearance Standard

Version 2. Effective March 1, 2025. Owner: Director of People. Approved by the Leadership Team on February 3, 2025. Replaces version 1.

## 1 The basics

Café staff wear the Halcyon apron on every shift, over clothes that are clean and free of holes. Tops are plain or simply patterned, with no slogans and no logo bigger than a playing card. Pants, jeans, skirts, and shorts are all fine; skirts and shorts reach at least mid-thigh. Leggings are fine under a skirt or with a top that covers the hips. HQ staff dress for their day and wear an apron only when working in a café.

## 2 Aprons

Every new café hire receives two aprons. The Café Manager replaces one apron a year for free; after that, a replacement costs $12, deducted from pay only with the employee's written consent. Aprons go home for washing and are never worn while working anywhere else. Shift Leads wear the charcoal apron, and everyone else wears the green one.

## 3 Shoes

Wear closed-toe shoes with slip-resistant soles and uppers that liquid can't soak through, so no mesh or canvas. After 90 days of employment, Halcyon reimburses up to $60 a year toward slip-resistant work shoes, with the receipt submitted through the staff app.

## 4 Hair and hats

Hair longer than shoulder length is tied back while working on bar or with food. Halcyon caps, plain caps, beanies, and head coverings worn for religious reasons are all welcome. Beards are fine; anyone with a beard longer than half an inch wears a beard net while plating food.

## 5 Jewelry, nails, and piercings

Behind the bar, the only jewelry worn on the hands or wrists is a plain band ring. Watches, bracelets, and fitness trackers come off before the shift, except medical alert bracelets. Earrings, facial piercings, and visible tattoos are fine as long as nothing hangs loose enough to catch on equipment and no tattoo is offensive. Keep nails short and clean; anyone wearing nail polish or artificial nails wears gloves to handle food that won't be cooked.

## 6 Name tags and pins

Name tags are optional and can show a chosen name. Staff may wear one pronoun pin and up to two other small pins on the apron, as long as none is political campaign material or advertises another business.

## 7 Accommodations

Requests for a religious, medical, or disability accommodation go to the Café Manager or straight to the People Team, and are answered within 10 days. Nobody has to explain more than is needed to make the change.
