# Paid Time Off and Leave Policy

Version 2. Effective January 1, 2024. Owner: Director of People. Approved by the Leadership Team on November 14, 2023. Replaces version 1.

## 1 Who this policy covers

This policy covers every Halcyon Coffee Co. employee at the cafés, the roastery, and HQ. Contractors and agency temps are not covered. "Hourly staff" means anyone paid by the hour, including Shift Leads and Assistant Café Managers. "Salaried staff" means Café Managers, District Managers, and salaried roastery and HQ staff.

## 2 Earning paid time off

Hourly staff earn 1 hour of paid time off (PTO) for every 40 hours worked, starting on their first day. Overtime hours count toward accrual. Salaried staff receive 15 days of PTO a year, credited in equal amounts each pay period.

PTO balances are capped at 60 hours for hourly staff and 160 hours for salaried staff. Once a balance reaches the cap, no more PTO is earned until some of it is used.

## 3 Requesting time off

Request PTO in the scheduling app at least 14 days before the first day off. The Café Manager, or an HQ employee's manager, approves or declines the request within 5 days, in the order requests were received. No PTO may be taken between November 25 and January 2 without District Manager approval.

## 4 Sick days

Sick days come out of the PTO balance; there is no separate sick time balance. Call the person in charge at least 2 hours before a shift you'll miss. After 3 consecutive missed shifts, a note from a health care provider is required before returning to work.

## 5 Bereavement leave

Employees receive up to 3 paid days off after the death of a spouse or partner, child, parent, sibling, or grandparent. Up to 2 more unpaid days may be added with the manager's approval.

## 6 Jury duty

Halcyon pays regular wages for up to 5 scheduled working days of jury duty a year. Show your manager the summons as soon as you receive it.

## 7 Parental leave

After 1 year of service, employees receive 4 weeks of paid parental leave at full pay for the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child. The leave must be taken within 6 months of the child's arrival.

## 8 Leaving Halcyon

Unused PTO is paid out on the final paycheck only when the employee has given at least two weeks' notice. Unused PTO is never paid out to an employee who is dismissed for misconduct.
