# Workplace Safety and Injury Reporting

Version 3. Effective February 1, 2026. Owner: Safety and Facilities Manager. Approved by the Leadership Team on January 12, 2026. Replaces version 2.

## 1 Everyone's part

Anyone who sees something unsafe stops the work and tells the person in charge. No one is disciplined for raising a safety concern in good faith. Each café keeps a first aid kit and a burn kit under the back counter and a fire extinguisher by the back door; the opening Shift Lead checks all three every Monday.

## 2 Burns and scalds

Steam wands, hot water spouts, and fresh coffee all scald. Cool a burn under cool running water for 20 minutes, and never put ice, butter, or cream on it. Take off rings and anything tight near the burn before it swells. Get medical care for any burn bigger than the injured person's palm, any blistering burn on the face, hands, feet, or a joint, and any chemical burn.

## 3 Cuts

Wash the cut, press on it, and cover it with a blue bandage from the first aid kit. Anyone with a bandage on a hand wears a glove over it for the rest of the shift. A cut that is still bleeding after 10 minutes of firm pressure needs medical care.

## 4 Lifting and carrying

Keep loads close and lift with your legs. Anything over 50 pounds (23 kg) is a two-person lift or goes on a cart. At the roastery, bags of green coffee are moved only with a hand truck or a forklift, and only certified operators drive forklifts.

## 5 Slips and spills

Put out a wet floor sign as soon as anything spills, and clean it up right away. Floor mats stay in place behind the bar during service. Report a loose tile, mat, or step to the Café Manager the same day.

## 6 Reporting an injury

### 6.1 Telling the person in charge

Report every work injury, however small, to the person in charge before the end of the shift, even if it seems fine at the time. The person in charge writes it up as an incident report in the safety portal within 24 hours.

### 6.2 Serious injuries

For a serious injury, call 911 first. A serious injury is one that needs more than first aid: stitches, a broken bone, a burn that needs medical care, a head injury, or any loss of consciousness. Then call the District Manager, who tells the Safety and Facilities Manager within 2 hours.

### 6.3 Getting treatment

Treatment for a work injury is paid through Halcyon's workers' compensation insurance. For care that isn't an emergency, go to an urgent care clinic and take the treatment referral form printed from the incident report. Don't use personal health insurance for a work injury.

## 7 Robbery and threats

If someone demands money or threatens you, do what they ask, stay calm, and never chase or fight anyone. Once they're gone, lock the doors, call 911, and then call the District Manager. Don't discuss the amount taken with guests or on social media.

## 8 Coming back after an injury

Anyone returning to work after a work injury brings a note from the treating provider listing any restrictions. The Café Manager arranges duties that fit them, such as register or prep work, for as long as the provider says.
