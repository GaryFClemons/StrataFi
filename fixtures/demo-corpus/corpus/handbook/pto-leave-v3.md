# Paid Time Off and Leave Policy

Version 3. Effective January 1, 2026. Owner: Director of People. Approved by the Leadership Team on November 18, 2025. Replaces version 2.

## 1 Who this policy covers

This policy covers every Halcyon Coffee Co. employee at the cafés, the Fenwick Street roastery, and HQ. Contractors and agency temps are not covered. "Hourly staff" means anyone paid by the hour, including Shift Leads and Assistant Café Managers. "Salaried staff" means Café Managers, District Managers, and salaried roastery and HQ staff. Where a city or state law gives more leave than this policy, the law applies.

## 2 Paid time off

### 2.1 How PTO accrues

Hourly staff accrue 1 hour of PTO for every 30 hours worked, from their first day. Overtime and paid training hours count toward accrual; PTO hours themselves don't. Salaried staff accrue 144 hours (18 days) a year, credited in equal amounts each pay period.

### 2.2 Balance cap

An hourly employee's PTO balance can hold up to 80 hours, and a salaried employee's up to 200 hours. At the cap, accrual pauses until the balance drops below it again; nothing already earned is lost.

### 2.3 Requesting PTO

Submit requests in the scheduling app. A request for 3 or more consecutive days off needs at least 21 days' notice, and a shorter request needs at least 7 days' notice. The Café Manager, or an HQ employee's manager, answers within 3 days. When two requests at one café conflict, the one submitted first is approved first.

### 2.4 Peak-season blackout

From December 15 through December 31, café staff may take PTO only with their District Manager's approval. The blackout doesn't apply to HQ or roastery staff, or to sick time.

## 3 Paid sick time

Sick time is kept separately from PTO. Every employee earns 1 hour of paid sick time for every 30 hours worked, up to 56 hours a year, and can use it from their 30th day of employment. Up to 40 unused hours carry over into the next calendar year.

Sick time covers your own illness, injury, or medical appointments; caring for an ill child, partner, parent, or other relative; and time off related to domestic violence. Tell the person in charge as early as you can, and at least 2 hours before the shift starts. A note from a health care provider is needed only after 3 or more consecutive missed shifts. The rules for coming back after vomiting or diarrhea are in the Food Safety and Allergen Standard.

## 4 Bereavement leave

After the death of a spouse or partner, child, stepchild, parent, stepparent, sibling, grandparent, or grandchild, employees receive up to 5 paid days off. After the death of an aunt, uncle, niece, nephew, cousin, or parent-in-law, they receive up to 2 paid days off. Up to 5 more unpaid days can be added with the manager's approval. The days don't have to be consecutive but must be used within 60 days of the death.

## 5 Jury duty and voting

Halcyon pays regular wages for up to 10 scheduled working days of jury duty a year. Give your manager a copy of the summons within 3 days of receiving it, and come in for the rest of any shift if you're released early enough to work at least 2 hours of it. Employees whose schedule leaves them fewer than 2 free hours while the polls are open may take up to 2 paid hours to vote.

## 6 Parental leave

Employees with at least 6 months of service receive 8 weeks of paid parental leave at full pay after the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child. It can be taken all at once or in blocks of at least one week, any time within 12 months of the child's arrival. Give 30 days' notice when the date can be foreseen.

## 7 Volunteer day

After 90 days of employment, each employee gets one paid volunteer day per calendar year, of 8 hours or the length of their usual shift, for work with a registered nonprofit. Request it in the scheduling app like PTO.

## 8 Leaving Halcyon

Up to 80 hours of unused PTO is paid out on the final paycheck, whatever notice is given. Sick time and the volunteer day are never paid out. An employee who is rehired within 6 months gets their unused sick time back.
