# Scheduling and Shift Swap Policy

Version 4. Effective March 1, 2026. Owner: Director of Café Operations. Approved by the Leadership Team on February 9, 2026. Replaces version 3.

## 1 The schedule

The café week runs Monday through Sunday. Café Managers post each week's schedule in the scheduling app at least 14 days before the week begins. Every shift on the schedule shows its start and end time, its station (bar, register, or floor), and who is in charge.

## 2 Availability

Staff set their availability in the scheduling app. A change takes effect 21 days after it's submitted, so it can be built into the next schedules. Part-time café staff stay available for at least 3 shifts a week, including one Saturday or Sunday shift. Students can ask for reduced availability for up to 4 weeks around exams.

## 3 Shift swaps

### 3.1 Trading a shift with a coworker

Post the shift on the app's swap board and choose the coworker who will take it. The app approves the swap automatically when three things are true: both people work at the same café, the coworker's training level is the same or higher, and the swap is posted at least 48 hours before the shift starts. Any other swap needs the Café Manager's approval in the app; Shift Leads can't approve swaps. Until the app shows the swap as approved, the shift is still yours.

### 3.2 What a swap can't do

A swap can't put anyone over 40 hours in the week, or leave anyone fewer than 10 hours between the end of one shift and the start of the next. It can't move a Level 1 barista onto an opening or closing shift.

### 3.3 Picking up open shifts

Open shifts are posted on the swap board. Level 2 and higher baristas may pick up open shifts at any café in their district; Level 1 baristas only at their home café. The first qualified person to claim an open shift gets it.

## 4 Breaks and rest

Staff get a paid 10-minute break for every 4 hours worked, and an unpaid 30-minute meal break on any shift longer than 6 hours, starting no later than the end of the fifth hour. The person in charge staggers breaks so the bar is never left unstaffed. Allow at least 10 hours between the end of a closing shift and the start of the next shift, unless the employee asks in writing for less; they can withdraw that request at any time.

## 5 Missing a shift

If you can't work a scheduled shift, call the person in charge or the Café Manager at least 2 hours before it starts. A text message or a swap-board post isn't notice. Missing a shift without any notice is a no-call, no-show, and three consecutive no-call, no-show shifts are treated as a resignation.

## 6 Changes to a posted schedule

When Halcyon changes a posted shift with less than 7 days' notice, the employee receives 1 extra hour of pay for each changed shift, unless they agreed to the change in the app first. When Halcyon cancels a shift with less than 24 hours' notice, the employee is paid for half of the scheduled hours, and never less than 2 hours. Swaps and pickups the employee asks for don't count as changes.

## 7 Overtime

Hours over 40 in a week are paid at 1.5 times the regular rate. The Café Manager approves overtime in advance, except when the person in charge keeps someone late for a safety or staffing emergency; those hours are paid, and the Café Manager is told before the end of the day.
