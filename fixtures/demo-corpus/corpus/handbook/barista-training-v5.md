# Barista Training and Certification Program

Version 5. Effective May 1, 2026. Owner: Director of Coffee. Approved by the Leadership Team on April 6, 2026. Replaces version 4.

## 1 The levels

### 1.1 Level 1: Barista in Training

Every new café hire starts at Level 1. In their first 30 days, a Level 1 barista completes the eight Foundations modules in the training app and at least 16 hours of shadowing a Coffee Trainer on bar. Level 1 baristas work the bar only alongside a Level 2 or higher barista, and they aren't scheduled to open or close.

### 1.2 Level 2: Certified Barista

To reach Level 2, a barista needs at least 45 days of employment, a score of 85% or higher on the written coffee exam, and a pass on the bar practical: six drinks from the standard menu in 12 minutes, each one within the Espresso and Brew Standards. Most baristas certify within their first 90 days.

### 1.3 Level 3: Senior Barista

After at least 6 months at Level 2, a barista can test for Level 3. The test has three parts: a cupping exam, picking the odd cup out in at least 5 of 6 triangulation sets; a latte art practical, pouring a heart, a tulip, and a rosetta in one sitting; and a dial-in practical, bringing a grinder from a deliberately wrong setting into the recipe range within 10 minutes.

### 1.4 Coffee Trainer

A Level 3 barista who has held Level 3 for at least 3 months and is recommended by their Café Manager can attend the two-day Train-the-Trainer workshop at the roastery. Coffee Trainers lead shadowing shifts and run the bar practical for Level 2.

## 2 Pay with each level

Reaching Level 2 brings a raise of $1.00 an hour, and reaching Level 3 another $1.25 an hour, from the first full pay period after passing. Coffee Trainers earn a premium of $1.50 an hour for time spent training others, logged in the training app.

## 3 Exams and retakes

The written exam can be retaken after 7 days, and a practical after 14 days. A barista who fails the same exam or practical three times in 6 months meets with their Café Manager to agree on a training plan before trying again.

## 4 Keeping a level

Level 2 and Level 3 baristas recertify every 12 months with a 20-minute calibration check run by a Coffee Trainer: two espresso shots and one milk drink, all to standard. A barista whose recertification is more than 30 days overdue keeps their pay but is scheduled at the level below until they pass.

## 5 Training time is paid

All training is paid at the regular rate, including the training app's modules. Do modules on shift at the café, or at home with the Café Manager's approval first, and log the time in the training app. Travel to the roastery for workshops is paid time, and mileage is reimbursed under the HQ Remote Work and Expense Policy.
