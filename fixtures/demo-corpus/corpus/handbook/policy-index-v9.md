# Policy Index and Owners

Version 9. Effective September 15, 2026. Owner: Director of People. Approved by the Leadership Team on September 8, 2026. Replaces version 8.

## 1 How the knowledge base is organized

Halcyon's written standards sit on four shelves. The Handbook covers working here: time off, pay practices, conduct, safety, and training. Operations covers how the cafés, the roastery, and wholesale run day to day. Management covers people and money decisions and is open only to Assistant Café Managers and above. Executive papers are for the executive team. Each document's first line gives its version, the date it took effect, and its owner. When there are several versions, follow the newest one that has already taken effect.

## 3 When nothing here answers the question

Café staff bring the question to their Café Manager, who finds the right owner in section 2 and gets an answer. HQ staff ask the owner directly. Never guess at a rule on food safety, cash, or pay; wait for the owner's answer.

## 4 Changing a document

Owners review their documents at least once a year. The Leadership Team approves every change, which is announced in the staff app at least 30 days before it takes effect whenever possible, and filed with a new version number.
