# HQ Remote Work and Expense Policy

Version 2. Effective April 1, 2026. Owner: Controller. Approved by the Leadership Team on March 9, 2026. Replaces version 1.

## 1 Who this policy covers

Sections 2 and 3 cover staff based at HQ, in the offices above the Fenwick Street roastery: finance, people, marketing, digital, wholesale sales, and coffee quality. Café staff, District Managers, and roastery production staff work on site. Sections 4 to 6 apply to anyone who travels or spends money for Halcyon.

## 2 Hybrid work

HQ staff work in the office on Tuesdays and Thursdays, the anchor days, and may work remotely on the other days. A fully remote arrangement needs approval from the employee's department head and the Director of People, and is reviewed every 6 months. Remote work is done from within the country; working from abroad for any length of time needs the Director of People's approval in advance.

## 3 Home office support

HQ staff on a hybrid or remote arrangement can claim a one-time home office reimbursement of up to $400 for a desk, chair, or monitor, and receive a $40 monthly internet allowance through payroll. Laptops and headsets come from IT and are returned when the employee leaves.

## 4 Travel

### 4.1 Booking

Book flights and hotels through the travel portal, at least 14 days ahead when you can. Flights are economy class, though a flight scheduled for more than 6 hours may be booked in premium economy. Hotels are capped at $225 a night before taxes.

### 4.2 Driving

Driving your own car on Halcyon business is reimbursed at 70 cents a mile, counted from your usual workplace or from home, whichever is closer to the destination. Parking and tolls are reimbursed with receipts. Traffic and parking tickets are never reimbursed.

### 4.3 Meals

On an overnight trip, meals are reimbursed up to $65 a day. Alcohol isn't reimbursed, except at a client meal a department head approved in advance.

## 5 Submitting expenses

Submit expenses in the expense app within 30 days of the purchase, with an itemized receipt for every expense of $25 or more. The employee's manager approves expenses up to $1,000; an expense over $1,000 also needs the Controller's approval. Expenses submitted more than 90 days after the purchase aren't reimbursed.

## 6 Company cards

Department heads and staff who travel often may hold a Halcyon purchasing card with a $2,500 monthly limit. Personal purchases on a company card aren't allowed, even if they're paid back.
