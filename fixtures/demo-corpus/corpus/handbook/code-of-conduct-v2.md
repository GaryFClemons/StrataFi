# Code of Conduct

Version 2. Effective January 15, 2025. Owner: Director of People. Approved by the Leadership Team on December 16, 2024. Replaces version 1.

## 1 How we treat each other

Treat guests, coworkers, and suppliers with respect. Disagree in private and without raising your voice. Halcyon doesn't tolerate bullying, threats, or violence from anyone, guests included; the person in charge may ask any guest who threatens staff to leave.

## 2 Harassment and discrimination

Harassment and discrimination based on race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, age, disability, or any other protected trait are prohibited. Report it to your Café Manager, your District Manager, the People Team, or the anonymous ethics line in the staff app. A manager who receives a report passes it to the People Team within 1 business day.

## 3 Staff drinks and discount

### 3.1 On shift

On shift, staff may have one handcrafted drink for every 4 hours scheduled, plus unlimited drip coffee, tea, and water. Ring every staff drink through the register with the staff drink key, even when it's free.

### 3.2 Off shift

Off shift, staff get 30% off drinks and food and 40% off whole-bean coffee at any Halcyon café, by showing the staff barcode in the staff app. The discount covers the employee's own order only. Friends and family pay full price, and ringing the discount in for anyone else is misuse of company property.

## 4 Gifts

Staff may keep small gifts from guests, such as a card or a treat. A gift from a supplier or a wholesale customer worth more than $50 is declined or reported to the People Team, and cash from a supplier is always declined.

## 5 Conflicts of interest

Tell the People Team if a relative or partner works in your reporting line, or if you have a financial stake in a supplier or wholesale customer. You may also work for another coffee business, but you may not share Halcyon recipes, roast profiles, supplier prices, or customer lists.

## 6 Social media and the press

You may say you work at Halcyon, but you don't speak for it. Don't post photos of guests without their consent, or anything from behind the bar that shows the safe, the cash office, or a document. Refer reporters to the Marketing Director.

## 7 Alcohol and drugs

Don't work under the influence of alcohol, cannabis, or any drug that impairs you. Tell the person in charge if a prescribed medication affects your ability to work safely.

## 8 Speaking up

Report a suspected breach of this code to a manager, the People Team, or the ethics line. Retaliating against anyone who reports in good faith is itself a breach of this code and grounds for termination.
