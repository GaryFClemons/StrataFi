# Refunds, Remakes and Guest Recovery

Version 3. Effective November 1, 2025. Owner: Director of Café Operations. Approved by the Leadership Team on October 13, 2025. Replaces version 2.

## 1 Remakes

If a guest isn't happy with a drink or food item for any reason, remake it once, free, without asking them to prove anything. Ring it through the register with the remake key so the waste is counted. A second remake on the same order needs the person in charge, who usually offers a different drink or a refund instead.

## 2 Refunds

### 2.1 Approval limits

Refunds go back to the original payment method. Each role can approve a refund up to a limit per transaction:

- Baristas: up to $15
- Shift Leads: up to $50
- Assistant Café Managers and Café Managers: up to $150
- Over $150: the District Manager, by phone or in the register

### 2.2 Cash and gift cards

Cash refunds are given only for purchases paid in cash. Purchases paid with a gift card are refunded onto a gift card. Gift card balances are never paid out in cash, and a lost gift card can be replaced only if the guest registered it in the Halcyon app.

## 3 Whole-bean coffee and merchandise

Unopened whole-bean coffee and unused merchandise can be returned within 30 days for a full refund with a receipt, or for store credit at the current price without one. An opened bag of coffee the guest doesn't enjoy can be exchanged once for a different coffee of the same or lower price.

## 4 Guest recovery cards

When something goes wrong beyond a single drink, such as a long wait, a wrong order on a busy morning, or a drink spilled on a guest, the person in charge can give a recovery card good for one free drink of any size. Recovery cards expire 60 days after they're given. A Shift Lead can give up to 3 per shift; more need the Café Manager.

## 5 Mobile orders

Problems with mobile orders are handled the same way as counter orders, except that refunds are made in the order tablet, not the register. Refunds to cards and the app take 5 to 10 business days to show up. Unclaimed orders are covered by the Mobile Order and Pay Procedures.

## 6 Damage and injury claims

If a guest says a drink damaged their clothing or belongings, or that they were hurt by a hot drink, don't admit fault or offer money. Take their name and contact details, file an incident report in the safety portal, and tell the Café Manager the same day.
