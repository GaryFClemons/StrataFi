# Halcyon Rewards Program

Version 3. Effective September 1, 2025. Owner: Digital and Loyalty Manager. Approved by the Leadership Team on August 4, 2025. Replaces version 2.

## 1 Joining

Guests join Halcyon Rewards for free in the Halcyon app, or at the register with an email address. They must be 13 or older. Each person can have one Rewards account, and points can't be moved between accounts.

## 2 Earning points

Guests earn 1 point for every $1 spent before tax on drinks, food, and retail coffee at the cafés and in the app. No points are earned on gift card purchases or reloads, tips, catering service charges, wholesale orders, or anything bought with the staff discount. Points post within 24 hours.

## 3 Rewards

At 60 points, a guest can redeem a free drink of any size with up to two modifiers. At 120 points, they can redeem a 12 oz bag of any whole-bean coffee. Rewards are redeemed in the app or by scanning the app at the register, and are never exchanged for cash.

## 4 Point expiry

Points expire 12 months after the end of the month in which they were earned, and the oldest points are spent first. The app warns guests 30 days before any of their points expire.

## 5 Birthday reward

Guests who add their birthday to the app at least 30 days ahead get a free drink of any size, valid for 7 days starting on their birthday.

## 6 Gold status

Guests who earn 500 points in a calendar year reach Gold status for the rest of that year and all of the next. Gold guests get free non-dairy milk and a free extra shot on every drink.

## 7 Missing points

If points haven't posted after 48 hours, the person in charge can add them in the register within 30 days of the purchase, with the receipt, up to 50 points per adjustment. Larger adjustments go to the Digital and Loyalty Manager through the staff app.
