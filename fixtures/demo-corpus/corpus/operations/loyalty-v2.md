# Halcyon Rewards Program

Version 2. Effective June 1, 2024. Owner: Digital and Loyalty Manager. Approved by the Leadership Team on May 6, 2024. Replaces version 1.

## 1 Joining

Guests join Halcyon Rewards for free in the Halcyon app. They must be 13 or older.

## 2 Earning stars

Guests earn 1 star for each visit with a purchase of $3 or more, scanned with the app at the register or placed as a mobile order. Only one star is earned per visit, whatever the total. Gift card purchases don't earn stars, and staff don't earn stars on purchases made with the staff discount.

## 3 Rewards

Ten stars earn a free drink of any size, with any modifiers. The reward appears in the app as soon as the tenth star posts. A guest can hold up to 3 unused free drink rewards at a time.

## 4 Expiry

Stars expire 6 months after they're earned. An unused free drink reward expires 30 days after it's issued.

## 5 Birthday treat

Guests who add their birthday in the app get a free pastry on their birthday, claimable that day only.

## 6 Double-star days

On the first Monday of each month, guests earn 2 stars for each qualifying visit.

## 7 Fixing missing stars

If a star hasn't posted after 24 hours, the person in charge can add it in the register within 14 days of the visit, with the receipt. Stars can be added this way for one guest no more than twice a month.

## 8 Leaving the program

Guests can close their Rewards account in the app at any time. Stars and rewards on a closed account are lost.
