# Equipment Cleaning Schedule

Version 3. Effective February 15, 2026. Owner: Quality and Food Safety Manager. Approved by the Leadership Team on January 26, 2026. Replaces version 2.

## 1 Using the schedule

Sign off every task below on the cleaning log with the date, the time, and your initials. If a task can't be done, for example because a part is missing, write the reason on the log and tell the Café Manager before the end of the shift. Wear gloves and eye protection when handling espresso machine detergent and descaler.

## 2 The schedule

| Equipment | Task | How often | Who |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Espresso machine | Backflush each group head with plain water | Every 2 hours during service | Barista on bar |
| Espresso machine | Backflush with detergent, then soak portafilters and baskets for 15 minutes | Daily at close | Closing barista |
| Steam wands | Purge and wipe with the wand cloth | After every drink | Barista on bar |
| Steam wands | Soak the tips in steam wand cleaner for 20 minutes | Daily at close | Closing barista |
| Grinders | Brush out the burrs and chute, and vacuum the hopper | Daily at close | Closing barista |
| Grinders | Run grinder cleaning tablets through | Weekly, at Sunday close | Closing Shift Lead |
| Batch brewers | Wash brew baskets, spray heads, and servers | Daily at close | Closing barista |
| Batch brewers | Descale | Monthly, on the first Monday | Opening Shift Lead |
| Blenders | Rinse, wash, and sanitize the jar | After each use | Barista on bar |
| Ice machine | Empty, wash, and sanitize the bin and scoop | Weekly, at Wednesday close | Closing Shift Lead |
| Ice machine | Full clean and descale | Every 6 months | Facilities contractor |
| Water filters | Replace the cartridges | Every 6 months, or sooner at 4,000 gallons | Facilities contractor |
| Refrigerators | Empty, wipe, and sanitize shelves; check door seals | Weekly, at Thursday close | Closing Shift Lead |
| Floor drains | Scrub and flush with drain cleaner | Weekly, at Saturday close | Closing barista |

## 3 Sanitizer

Mix quaternary sanitizer to 200–400 ppm, and check it with a test strip when it's mixed and every 4 hours after that. Change sanitizer buckets every 4 hours, or sooner if the water turns cloudy. Store chemicals below and away from food, cups, and milk.

## 4 Checks and records

The Café Manager reviews the cleaning log every week, and the District Manager checks it on each café visit, at least once a month. Logs are kept at the café for 90 days. A café that misses the same task twice in one month gets a follow-up visit from the Quality and Food Safety Manager.
