# Opening and Closing Procedures

Version 5. Effective April 1, 2026. Owner: Director of Café Operations. Approved by the Leadership Team on March 9, 2026. Replaces version 4.

## 1 Who opens and closes

At least two people open and close every café, and one of them is a Shift Lead or manager with keys and an alarm code. Nobody opens or closes alone. If the second person hasn't arrived, the keyholder waits in their car or another safe place and calls the Café Manager. Level 1 baristas aren't scheduled on opening or closing shifts.

## 2 Opening

### 2.1 Arriving

The opening crew arrives 60 minutes before the café opens; most cafés open at 6:30 a.m. Before going in, walk past the front and back doors. If a door is open, glass is broken, or anything else looks wrong, don't go in: get to a safe place, call 911, and then call the District Manager.

### 2.2 Opening checklist

1. Disarm the alarm within 60 seconds of opening the door.
2. Turn on the espresso machines and batch brewers; they need 30 minutes to reach temperature.
3. Read and record every fridge and freezer temperature.
4. Check date labels and throw out anything past its time.
5. Dial in each grinder and record it on the dial-in log.
6. The Shift Lead sets up the tills.
7. Turn on mobile ordering in the order tablet 15 minutes before opening.

### 2.3 If the café can't open on time

If the café won't be open within 15 minutes of its posted time, the Shift Lead calls the District Manager, puts up the "opening soon" sign, and keeps mobile ordering off until the café is ready.

## 3 Closing

### 3.1 Last orders

Take the last orders 10 minutes before closing time, and turn off mobile ordering 20 minutes before closing. Lock the front door at closing time. Guests already inside may finish their drinks, but no new orders are taken.

### 3.2 Closing checklist

1. Clean everything marked "Daily at close" on the Equipment Cleaning Schedule.
2. Empty the pastry case. Pastries in good condition go in the donation bin for the food rescue partner's morning pickup, and the rest are logged as waste. Staff may not take leftover pastries home.
3. Read and record fridge and freezer temperatures.
4. The Shift Lead counts down the tills, and counts the tip jar with another closer.
5. Check the restrooms and storerooms to make sure nobody is still inside.
6. Set the alarm and leave together. Walk to cars or the bus stop as a group.

## 4 Alarms and lost keys

If the alarm company calls about an alarm after hours, the Café Manager or the on-call Shift Lead decides whether to go to the café, and never goes in alone or before the police have checked the building. A keyholder who loses keys tells the Café Manager the same day, and the locks are changed within 48 hours.
