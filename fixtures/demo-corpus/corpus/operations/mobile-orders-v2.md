# Mobile Order and Pay Procedures

Version 2. Effective January 15, 2026. Owner: Digital and Loyalty Manager. Approved by the Leadership Team on December 15, 2025. Replaces version 1.

## 1 How orders arrive

Mobile orders print on the label printer at the hand-off end of the bar and appear on the order tablet with the guest's first name and last initial. The app quotes a ready time of 5 to 12 minutes, based on the café's queue at that moment. Mobile ordering opens 15 minutes before the café does and closes 20 minutes before closing time.

## 2 Making mobile orders

Make mobile and counter orders in the order they come in. Don't hold a mobile order back until the guest arrives, and don't let it jump the line. Each drink gets its printed label, and an order with more than one item goes in a bag or carrier with the receipt stapled to it.

## 3 The pickup shelf

Put finished orders on the pickup shelf and mark them ready in the order tablet, which notifies the guest. Hot drinks stay on the shelf for 20 minutes after they're marked ready, and cold drinks and food for 30 minutes. After that, log the order as unclaimed in the order tablet and discard it. If the guest turns up later the same day with the order in their app, remake it free.

## 4 Pausing mobile ordering

The person in charge can pause mobile ordering in the order tablet for up to 30 minutes when more than 15 drinks are waiting or the café is short-staffed. A pause longer than 30 minutes, or a third pause in one day, needs the Café Manager's approval.

## 5 Delivery couriers

Orders from delivery apps arrive on the same tablet and join the same queue. Hand an order to a courier only after they give the guest's name and the order code shown on the tablet. If a delivery order is still waiting 15 minutes after it's marked ready, report it to the delivery partner through the tablet; don't remake it unless the partner asks.

## 6 Wrong or missing items

Fix mistakes the same way as at the counter, under Refunds, Remakes and Guest Recovery. Mobile order refunds are made in the order tablet by a Shift Lead or a manager; baristas can't refund mobile orders.
